The journey, without hindsight bias

Separate what was historically documented from what we are proposing now.

Original venture | 2015–2020

Deliver energy to stranded EVs

Original truck-as-rental-power-plant; booked on-demand; solar parks/hubs; priority subscriptions; power rental and road resilience. The archived deck also describes alternatives to diesel generators and emergency/CSR use.

New scope | 2026

Manage an inventory of stored electricity

Swappable large BESS powers fixed DC–DC chargers; compatible small packs supply electric riders; mobile trucks distribute energy; an optimizer targets time, place, capacity and customer reliability.

How the ideas emerged

1. Range anxiety → mobile supply

Instead of assuming universal fixed coverage, dispatch a rechargeable energy asset to the customer.

2. Transformer bottlenecks → station BESS

High charging peaks can be buffered using interchangeable storage, subject to safe DC–DC integration.

3. Rider downtime → battery swaps

Use compatible pack families, on-site monitored cabinets and doorstep exchanges.

4. Price variation → arbitrage

Charge at economically viable times/locations and deliver energy where utilization and service value are highest.

5. Host demand → asset-light distribution

Partner with existing destinations rather than build every stand-alone charging site from zero.

6. Investor feedback → one pilot

Turn claims into measurements: kWh, margin, repeat usage, safety, uptime, LOIs and signed terms.

Pitch and rehearsal

Choose the script length depending on the room. Iman presents the venture, the team supplies the evidence, and nobody presents a concept as operating hardware.

Twenty seconds

“EV charging infrastructure cannot always be installed where demand appears. Bijli Haazir makes stored power dispatchable: by truck, by swappable battery banks feeding fixed DC–DC chargers, and by pack swaps for riders.”


One minute

“Pakistan's EV transition faces a location problem, a power-capacity problem and a downtime problem. Our original Bijli Haazir concept tackled stranded vehicles with mobile, battery-backed charging. The next generation brings three services into one energy network: energy trucks, fixed charging points buffered by interchangeable large battery banks, and compatible motorcycle battery swaps. We aim to source electricity from solar and commercially viable grid windows, then dispatch storage where customer demand justifies the full cost. Our first goal is not a nationwide network. It is one verified hub, one host site and one rider fleet proving safety, uptime, delivered kWh, repeat usage and positive contribution.”


Three-minute pitch outline

0:00–0:30 · Why now

Charging gaps, costly electrical upgrades, idle rider time and energy price/location mismatch.

0:30–1:00 · Track record

Zorays Solar since 2014, i2i, Startup Istanbul directory, patent application, archived mobile truck proposition.

1:00–1:45 · System

Solar/grid hub → measured battery inventory → truck, fixed-site DC–DC BESS, rider battery swaps. Dispatch optimizer.

1:45–2:20 · Economics

Time/place/service arbitrage; illustrate 100 kWh example and immediately disclose that gross spread ≠ profit.

2:20–2:45 · Pilot

One hub/site/fleet; compliance, cost, uptime, demand, subscription repeat usage.

2:45–3:00 · Ask

Host, integrator, fleet, incubation and grant/prototype validation support.
