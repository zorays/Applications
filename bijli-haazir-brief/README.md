# Bijli Haazir — Team Briefing & Flashcards

Public, mobile-friendly team briefing for the **20–21 November 2026 LUMS CleanTech Hackathon**.

**Start here:** [Open the interactive briefing file](./index.html). It contains 42 flashcards, 24 falsifiable postulates, a milestone timeline, three pitch versions, Gust investor-readiness notes, and source/video links.

## Our positioning

**Bijli Haazir** brings together (a) the mobile charging idea shown in the archived Startup Istanbul pitch; (b) modular large battery banks supplying fixed DC–DC stations; (c) compatible motorcycle pack swapping; and (d) temporal, spatial and service-value energy arbitrage.

**2026 status:** Expanded product is pre-pilot. Claims should not imply live deployments, granted patent rights, signed commercial host agreements or verified unit economics.

## How the team should use it

1. Open `index.html` as a browser file locally (or through Pages when configured).
2. Review a topic's flashcards sequentially; tap to reveal the answer.
3. Work through Postulates and tick a claim only when the stated evidence gate is achieved.
4. Keep actual shared decisions in GitHub commits or Issues; browser checkboxes are **local to each user's device**.
5. Rehearse the 20-second, one-minute and three-minute pitches.
6. Cross-check sources. The 2020 venture deck and 2026 pitch deck were reviewed, but their source PDFs are not republished here.

## Investor discipline

The provided Gust evaluation recommended **friends & family / early-stage accelerators**; its $5k–$150k stage range is not an offer, valuation or numeric rank. A prototype, validated safety, signed host/fleet LOIs, repeat paid usage, and net contribution per kWh are the next evidence gates.
